# Dikdik 0.7.3 — Gemini works with your documents, and every drawing reads clearly

## Gemini: tasks with documents and data work
Tasks on Gemini that used any tool — reading an attached Word file or spreadsheet,
running statistics, searching — failed on their second step with "The provider refused
the request (HTTP 400)". Gemini signs each tool call and requires the signature back;
Dikdik dropped it. Signatures now go back unchanged, including in streamed replies,
after a long conversation is shortened, and when you resume a task.

## Drawings: placed, sized and coloured for reading
- **No overlapping labels.** In function plots and science sketches every label is put
  where it covers no other label and crosses no line, close to what it names. The
  drawing grows so no label is cut off, and each label has a thin outline in the board
  colour so a line passing behind never cuts through its letters.
- **Text that contrasts with its background** in every diagram, on the chalkboard,
  whiteboard and clean looks, light and dark — each pair is checked against the
  WCAG 4.5:1 standard. Fixed: the mind map's centre (white on yellow), pale bar charts
  with an invisible line, unreadable table rows in data diagrams, neon journey colours,
  and pie legends that did not match their slices.
- **Labels on lines sit on solid backgrounds**, so lines no longer run through words.
- **Larger diagram text**, Gantt charts at a comfortable width (no more cut-off bars),
  more compact flowcharts, and drawings that fit the card unless they are very wide.
