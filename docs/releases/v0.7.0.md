# Dikdik 0.7.0 — a brain and a tutor

Dikdik now explains like a teacher at the board and researches like a senior researcher.
Mathematics is properly typeset, processes and ideas are drawn by hand on a chalkboard,
functions are graphed exactly, and answers are backed by the web and the scholarly
literature. (0.6.0 was never published; everything from it ships here.)

## Drawing like a teacher
- **Chalkboard, whiteboard or clean.** Every drawing appears on a board in handwriting
  and is drawn in stroke by stroke the first time it appears. Switch style on any
  drawing or in Settings → General → *Drawing style*; Replay draws it again. Reduced
  motion settings skip the animation.
- **Diagrams** use the real Mermaid engine, bundled and loaded only when needed:
  flowcharts, sequence, class, state, entity-relationship, mind maps, timelines, Gantt,
  pie, quadrant, XY charts, journeys, Git graphs, Sankey and block diagrams.
- **Function plots** (```` ```plot ````): `y = x^2 - 4`, several curves, ranges with π,
  marked points and lines, curves broken at asymptotes. Computed exactly with a safe
  expression parser — nothing is executed.
- **Science sketches** (```` ```sketch ````): forces on a slope, projectile paths, circuits,
  geometry and cells from simple JSON — lines, vectors, circles, polygons, curves,
  angles, points and labels.
- **Worked solutions on the board** (```` ```board ````), revealed line by line.
- Copy source, SVG and PNG download (with the board look) and full-screen zoom.

## Formulas
- Typeset with KaTeX, bundled so it works offline: `$...$` and `\(...\)` inline;
  `$$...$$`, `\[...\]`, `\begin{align}` and ```` ```math ```` blocks as centred
  equations — in replies, tables, Markdown files and previews. In handwriting on boards.
- Prices ("$5 and $10"), shell variables and code are never mistaken for maths.
- Copy LaTeX on every display equation; screen readers read formulas through MathML.
- Word documents from `create_document` turn LaTeX into native, editable Word equations,
  and `read_document` reads them back.

## Research for doctors, researchers, teachers and everyone
- **`literature_search`** (no key needed): OpenAlex across every field, PubMed with
  abstracts for medicine (filter for reviews and trials, by year), arXiv preprints
  (labelled as not peer reviewed), and DOI verification with Crossref so invented
  references are caught.
- Chat searches the web and the literature before answering anything time-sensitive or
  specific, reads the best sources, cross-checks them and cites them.
- Clinicians get answers from current guidelines with doses, adjustments and red flags;
  researchers get supervisor-level design and statistics advice with real citations;
  teachers get ready-to-use lessons, exercises and marking schemes; learners get
  intuition, worked examples, a drawing and a practice question.

## Fixes
- A display formula followed by text on the same line (`$$...$$ ✓`) no longer swallows
  the lines after it.

## Upgrading
Nothing to configure. The drawing style defaults to Chalkboard.
