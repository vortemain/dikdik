# Dikdik 0.7.2 — replies that type out, chemistry and light mode

## Replies appear as they are written
Chat never streamed before — only Code did — so you waited for the whole answer before
seeing anything. Now a reply is typed onto the screen as the model writes it:
- A steady, readable pace that smooths out the bursts models send.
- Finished paragraphs, drawings and formulas are drawn once and stay put.
- Formulas, bold text, links and drawings appear whole — never raw `$\frac{`, `**` or code.
- The view follows the newest line unless you scroll up to read; reduced-motion settings
  show text as it arrives, without the animation.

## "Check your answer" reveals
`<details><summary>` blocks are a real collapsible card you click to open, instead of
raw tags in the text. Common inline HTML from models — `<b>`, `<i>`, `<br>`, `<sub>`,
`<sup>`, `<kbd>` — becomes proper formatting. Conversation titles no longer keep
Markdown such as `##`.

## Long answers are shown in full
Replies longer than 8,000 characters were cut off in the conversation with
"…(truncated)", even though the whole reply had been saved. Full lessons, reports and
long messages are now shown to the end.

## Chemistry that looks like chemistry
- Chemistry is typeset with KaTeX's mhchem extension: formulas (`\ce{CH3-CH2-OH}`),
  ions (`\ce{SO4^2-}`), reactions (`\ce{2H2 + O2 -> 2H2O}`) and equilibria.
- Chemistry written as plain maths — `$CH_3-CH_3$`, `$H_2O$`, `$-OH$`, `$Cl$`,
  `$C_nH_{2n+2}$` — is shown with upright element symbols instead of italic algebra.
  Physics and statistics such as `$V = IR$`, `$P_1V_1 = P_2V_2$` and `$H_0$` are left alone.
- Chat now writes chemistry with `\ce{}`, in text and in tables.
- Bare numbers like `$3$` are typeset instead of showing their dollar signs; prices such
  as "$5 and $10" still stay text.

## Boards and copying
- Each line of a worked solution on the board is its own step again.
- Copying from a reply gives formulas as LaTeX, tables as tab-separated rows, and no
  button labels — paste into Word, a document or another chat cleanly.
- No more faint ghost letter after chemical formulas on the chalkboard.

## Light and dark mode
- Worked solutions on the chalkboard are readable in light mode (they had dark text on
  the green board), and on the whiteboard in dark mode.
- Clean-style drawings redraw when you switch between light and dark, so their labels
  never turn invisible.

## Security
- Mermaid is built from its npm package against Dikdik's patched KaTeX 0.18.11 (its
  prebuilt file embedded KaTeX 0.16.47). Dependency audits are clean.

## Upgrading
Nothing to configure.
